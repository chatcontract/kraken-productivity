## How to customise it

Replace each section below with your team's actual positioning, then delete the banner at the top. Call Prep will start folding it into *Risks to watch*, *What you need to know before the call*, and the framing of *What you should accomplish*.

Two rules on anything written here:

- **Positioning is framing, never fact.** Call Prep still sources every substantive claim from something it actually read — a transcript, an email, a CRM record. Nothing in this file becomes a citation, and nothing here overrides what the account's own history shows.
- **Keep it short.** Half a page per section. This is read on every run, so length costs time on every run.
